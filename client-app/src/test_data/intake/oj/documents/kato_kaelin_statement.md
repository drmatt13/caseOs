# WITNESS STATEMENT OF BRIAN "KATO" KAELIN

**Case Number:** BA097211
**Witness Statement Number:** WS-KK-001
**Witness Name:** Brian Gerard "Kato" Kaelin
**Occupation:** Actor / Television Personality
**Residence at Time of Events:** Guest House, 360 North Rockingham Avenue, Los Angeles, California
**Statement Date:** June 16, 1994
**Interview Conducted By:** Detective Philip Vannatter
**Assisting Investigator:** Detective Thomas Lange
**Recording Method:** Audio Recorded and Written Summary
**Related Subjects:** Orenthal James Simpson, Allan Park, Nicole Brown Simpson, Ronald Goldman

---

# WITNESS BACKGROUND

My name is Brian Kaelin, although most people know me as Kato Kaelin.

At the time of the events in question, I was residing in a guest house located on the Rockingham property owned by Orenthal James Simpson.

I had known Mr. Simpson socially and had been staying in the guest house with his permission.

The guest house is physically separated from the main residence by a walkway and landscaped areas. While I could observe certain portions of the property, I did not have visibility into every area.

I was familiar with normal sounds and activity patterns at the residence because I spent significant time there.

---

# EVENTS PRIOR TO THE EVENING

During the day of June 12, 1994, nothing stood out to me as unusual.

I observed routine activity at the property.

I do not recall any arguments, disturbances, or unusual visitors.

I was aware that Mr. Simpson planned to travel later that evening.

I understood that transportation arrangements had been made for an airport trip.

---

# EVENING ACTIVITIES

During the evening I spent time in and around the guest house.

My recollection is that I watched television for part of the evening.

I may have spoken briefly with Mr. Simpson earlier in the night, although I cannot confidently reconstruct the exact timing.

Many of my timing estimates are based on later attempts to reconstruct events rather than contemporaneous notes.

---

# INTERACTION WITH MR. SIMPSON

At some point during the evening I recall speaking with Mr. Simpson.

The conversation was routine.

I do not recall anything unusual about his demeanor.

He appeared to be occupied with ordinary activities and preparations associated with travel.

I did not observe visible injuries.

I did not conduct any close inspection of his appearance.

---

# GUEST HOUSE LOCATION AND CONDITIONS

The guest house is situated behind the main residence.

The area includes:

* Walkways
* Landscaping
* Trees
* Exterior lighting
* Utility structures

Certain portions of the pathway area can be heard more easily than they can be seen due to vegetation and building placement.

This became relevant later in the evening.

---

# OBSERVATION OF UNUSUAL SOUND

The event I remember most clearly occurred later in the evening while I was inside the guest house.

At some point I heard what I described as a "thump," "bump," or impact-type sound.

The noise attracted my attention because it seemed unusual.

---

## Approximate Timing

My best estimate is approximately 10:35 p.m. to 10:45 p.m.

I cannot identify a more precise time.

I have provided different estimates during different interviews because I am attempting to reconstruct the timing from memory.

Any exact minute would be speculation.

---

## Description of Sound

The sound was not extremely loud.

It was noticeable enough that I paused what I was doing.

I remember thinking it sounded like:

* Something striking a wall
* Something falling
* Something hitting an object

I cannot state which of those possibilities is correct.

---

## Duration

The sound was brief.

I do not recall repeated impacts.

I do not recall a prolonged disturbance.

---

# INVESTIGATION OF SOUND

After hearing the noise, I became curious and decided to investigate.

I exited the guest house and looked around portions of the property.

I specifically directed my attention toward the area from which I believed the sound originated.

---

## Observations During Inspection

While outside I observed:

* Normal property lighting
* Landscaping
* Walkways

I did not observe:

* A struggle
* Any injured person
* Any criminal activity
* Anyone running

I could not determine what caused the sound.

---

## Area of Concern

The sound appeared to originate from the direction of the rear pathway area located between portions of the main residence and guest house.

This is generally the same area where investigators later reported recovering a glove.

At the time, however, I did not observe a glove or any item that appeared significant.

---

# CONTACT WITH ALLAN PARK

At some point later in the evening I became aware of the presence of limousine driver Allan Park.

I either saw him directly or became aware that he was attempting to make contact with the residence.

My recollection is that transportation arrangements became a topic of discussion because Mr. Simpson was preparing to depart for the airport.

---

# OBSERVATIONS OF PROPERTY ACTIVITY

As the evening progressed, I observed routine travel preparations.

These included:

* Movement around the residence
* Luggage-related activity
* General preparation for departure

Nothing I observed at that time caused me to believe a serious crime had occurred elsewhere.

---

# OBSERVATION OF MR. SIMPSON LATER IN EVENING

I observed Mr. Simpson later in the evening before departure.

My recollection is that he appeared focused on travel arrangements.

I do not recall him appearing panicked or frantic.

I do not recall any statements suggesting an emergency.

I cannot provide precise times for these observations.

---

# MEDIA COVERAGE AND MEMORY RECONSTRUCTION

After the investigation became public, I was exposed to extensive media coverage.

I subsequently participated in multiple interviews with investigators, attorneys, and other parties.

As time passed, I attempted repeatedly to reconstruct the sequence of events.

Because of that process, I want to emphasize that certain details are remembered more clearly than others.

The sound I heard remains one of the clearest memories.

Exact timing remains much less certain.

---

# CLARIFICATIONS REGARDING THE SOUND

I wish to clarify the following:

### Point 1

I heard a sound that I believed was unusual.

---

### Point 2

I cannot identify the source of the sound.

---

### Point 3

I cannot identify any person associated with the sound.

---

### Point 4

I did not witness the event that produced the sound.

---

### Point 5

I cannot determine whether the sound was connected to this investigation.

---

### Point 6

I cannot determine whether the sound originated from:

* A person
* An animal
* An object
* Property equipment

---

# FOLLOW-UP INVESTIGATION DISCUSSIONS

During interviews, investigators suggested several possible explanations for the sound.

These included:

* Someone moving through the pathway area
* An object striking part of the structure
* Activity involving the guest house exterior

I was unable to confirm any of those possibilities.

My testimony is limited to hearing the sound itself and attempting to locate its source.

---

# TIMELINE ISSUES IDENTIFIED

Investigators noted several issues concerning timeline reconstruction.

---

## Issue A

The exact time of the sound remains uncertain.

---

## Issue B

No independent recording exists documenting the sound.

---

## Issue C

No witness observed the event producing the sound.

---

## Issue D

Subsequent recollections may have been influenced by media exposure and repeated questioning.

---

## Issue E

Different interviews contain slightly different time estimates.

---

# MATTERS RELEVANT TO FUTURE TESTIMONY

If called to testify, I expect my testimony would generally be limited to:

1. My presence at the Rockingham property.
2. My relationship to the residence.
3. The sound I heard near the guest house.
4. My attempt to investigate the sound.
5. My observations regarding airport preparation activity.
6. My interactions with Allan Park and others.
7. My inability to identify the source of the sound.

---

# FINAL STATEMENT

To the best of my recollection, I was present at the Rockingham property on the evening of June 12, 1994. At some point later in the evening, I heard an unusual impact-type sound from the direction of the pathway area near the guest house. I investigated but was unable to determine the source. I did not witness any criminal activity, did not observe any assault, and cannot identify any individual responsible for producing the sound. My timing estimates remain approximate and should not be interpreted as precise measurements.
