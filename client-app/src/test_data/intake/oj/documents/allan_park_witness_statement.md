# WITNESS STATEMENT OF ALLAN PARK

**Case Number:** BA097211
**Witness Statement Number:** WS-AP-001
**Witness Name:** Allan Park
**Occupation:** Limousine Driver
**Employer:** Executive Limousine Services, Inc.
**Statement Date:** June 17, 1994
**Interview Conducted By:** Detective Thomas Lange
**Location of Interview:** West Los Angeles Division Interview Room B
**Recording Method:** Audio Recorded and Written Summary
**Related Subjects:** Orenthal James Simpson, Nicole Brown Simpson, Ronald Goldman

---

# WITNESS BACKGROUND

My name is Allan Park. I am employed as a limousine driver with Executive Limousine Services. I have worked in transportation services for approximately twelve years. My duties primarily involve transporting clients to airports, business meetings, hotels, and private residences.

I was assigned to transport Orenthal James Simpson to Los Angeles International Airport on the evening of June 12, 1994, for a scheduled flight to Chicago.

Prior to that evening, I had transported Mr. Simpson on previous occasions. I was generally familiar with the location of his residence at 360 North Rockingham Avenue but did not have a personal relationship with him.

---

# ASSIGNMENT DETAILS

According to my dispatch records, I was instructed to arrive at Mr. Simpson's residence at approximately 10:45 p.m. to facilitate transportation to Los Angeles International Airport.

I reviewed my trip sheet prior to this interview. The assignment was considered routine.

I arrived driving a dark-colored limousine owned by Executive Limousine Services.

---

# ARRIVAL AT ROCKINGHAM

I believe I arrived in the area sometime between approximately 10:22 p.m. and 10:25 p.m.

I cannot state the exact minute without relying on company records, but I remember arriving somewhat earlier than the scheduled pickup time.

When I first arrived, I parked near the front of the property.

The residence appeared generally quiet.

I did not immediately observe Mr. Simpson.

I did not initially observe any unusual activity.

---

# INITIAL OBSERVATIONS

After parking, I remained in the vehicle for a short period.

I recall looking toward the property several times.

The front gate area appeared closed.

The property lighting appeared normal for a large residence.

I did not observe anyone entering or leaving through the front gate while I was first waiting.

I remember seeing lights on within portions of the residence.

I do not remember hearing any yelling, arguing, or unusual noises from inside the property.

---

# ATTEMPTS TO MAKE CONTACT

After waiting for a period of time, I attempted to contact the residence.

I used the intercom system.

I do not recall receiving an immediate response.

I made additional attempts.

I remember becoming concerned that either:

1. The pickup time had changed, or
2. Mr. Simpson was running late.

At that point I continued monitoring the residence and waiting for further activity.

---

# OBSERVATION OF FIGURE NEAR PROPERTY

One of the events I remember most clearly is observing what I initially believed to be a person moving near the property.

I cannot provide an exact time.

My best estimate is sometime after I had already been waiting several minutes.

I observed what appeared to be a dark figure or shadow-like individual near the driveway area.

The observation lasted only briefly.

Because it was nighttime and the individual was at a distance, I could not identify the person.

I could not determine:

* Height with certainty
* Weight with certainty
* Clothing details
* Facial characteristics

I remember believing someone had moved across part of the property.

I cannot state whether the individual came from inside the residence, outside the residence, or another area of the property.

---

# ESTIMATED TIMING OF OBSERVATION

I have attempted several times to reconstruct the timing.

My estimates have varied somewhat.

I believe the observation occurred somewhere between approximately 10:35 p.m. and 10:50 p.m.

I cannot be more precise than that.

Any narrower estimate would be speculation.

---

# LIGHTING CONDITIONS

The lighting conditions were not ideal.

Several portions of the property were illuminated.

Other areas were shadowed.

I was viewing the property from outside the gate.

My attention was divided between:

* Waiting for my passenger
* Monitoring traffic
* Watching the residence

I cannot say that I had an uninterrupted view of the property during the entire waiting period.

---

# TELEPHONE CONTACTS

At some point while waiting, I contacted dispatch.

I advised that I was present and waiting for my passenger.

I do not specifically recall the exact time of that communication.

Company records should reflect those details more accurately than my memory.

---

# SUBSEQUENT CONTACT WITH RESIDENCE

After additional waiting, activity began occurring at the property.

I eventually observed movement associated with preparation for departure.

I recall seeing individuals moving in and around the residence.

At some point, contact was established and I was informed that the airport transportation would proceed.

I no longer believed there was a problem with the pickup.

---

# OBSERVATION OF MR. SIMPSON

I eventually observed Mr. Simpson.

My recollection is that he appeared to be preparing for travel.

I observed luggage associated with the trip.

Nothing immediately struck me as unusual.

He appeared focused on departing for the airport.

I did not observe obvious injuries.

I did not conduct any inspection of his appearance.

My interaction was consistent with a transportation assignment rather than a law-enforcement investigation.

---

# LUGGAGE OBSERVATIONS

I observed luggage being loaded for transport.

I recall at least one larger bag.

I believe there were additional travel items, although I cannot confidently reconstruct every item.

I did not inspect the contents of any luggage.

I have no knowledge regarding what was packed inside.

---

# TRAVEL TO AIRPORT

Once departure preparations were complete, we proceeded toward Los Angeles International Airport.

The trip itself was generally uneventful.

Traffic conditions were normal for that time of night.

I do not recall significant delays.

Mr. Simpson appeared focused on catching his scheduled flight.

Our conversation was limited.

Most of the trip involved routine transportation.

---

# AIRPORT ARRIVAL

We arrived at Los Angeles International Airport with sufficient time for departure procedures.

I observed normal passenger drop-off activities.

Luggage was unloaded.

Mr. Simpson exited the vehicle.

I considered the assignment complete.

---

# SUBSEQUENT CONTACT BY INVESTIGATORS

After learning about the investigation, I was contacted by law enforcement.

Investigators asked me to reconstruct events from the evening.

I attempted to provide accurate information.

I explained repeatedly that certain timing estimates were approximations.

Several days had passed between the events and some interviews.

I reviewed dispatch information where available.

However, portions of my testimony remain dependent upon memory rather than contemporaneous notes.

---

# CLARIFICATIONS AND LIMITATIONS

I wish to make the following points clear:

### Point 1

I did observe what I believed was a person or figure moving on the property.

I could not identify that individual.

---

### Point 2

I cannot testify regarding where that individual came from.

---

### Point 3

I cannot testify regarding what that individual may have been doing.

---

### Point 4

My timing estimates should be considered approximate.

---

### Point 5

I did not witness any criminal activity.

---

### Point 6

I did not observe any confrontation or altercation.

---

### Point 7

I have no personal knowledge regarding events occurring at the Bundy residence.

---

# ISSUES IDENTIFIED DURING INTERVIEW

Investigators and witness discussed several areas requiring caution.

## Issue A

The exact time of the observed figure remains uncertain.

---

## Issue B

Lighting conditions may have affected perception.

---

## Issue C

Distance may have affected identification ability.

---

## Issue D

Media coverage occurring after the incident may influence retrospective recollection.

---

## Issue E

Subsequent interviews may have caused certain memories to become more fixed than they were originally.

---

# FINAL STATEMENT

To the best of my recollection, I arrived at the Rockingham residence for a scheduled airport pickup, waited for a period of time, observed what appeared to be a person moving on or near the property, eventually made contact regarding the transportation assignment, transported Mr. Simpson to Los Angeles International Airport, and completed the assignment without observing any criminal activity.

I have attempted to answer all questions truthfully and accurately. Where I have provided estimated times, those estimates reflect my best recollection and should not be interpreted as precise measurements.
