# PURPOSE OF MEMORANDUM

This memorandum reconstructs the events surrounding June 12–13, 1994 using currently available witness statements, telephone records, travel estimates, police reports, forensic reports, limousine driver observations, and investigative materials.

The objective is not to establish an alternative factual narrative, but to identify:

* Timeline inconsistencies
* Uncertainties in estimated time of death
* Witness conflicts
* Physical feasibility issues
* Investigative assumptions
* Areas where reasonable doubt may exist

This reconstruction should be considered preliminary and subject to revision as additional discovery materials become available.

---

# PROSECUTION THEORY (CURRENT UNDERSTANDING)

The prosecution appears expected to argue:

1. Nicole Brown Simpson and Ronald Goldman were killed outside the Bundy residence.
2. The murders occurred shortly before defendant's scheduled departure for Chicago.
3. Orenthal James Simpson traveled between Rockingham and Bundy.
4. Simpson committed the murders.
5. Simpson returned to Rockingham.
6. Simpson concealed evidence.
7. Simpson subsequently departed for Chicago.

The prosecution theory depends heavily upon:

* Time-of-death estimates
* Biological evidence
* Glove evidence
* Limousine observations
* Witness timing estimates
* Travel assumptions

# PRELIMINARY DEFENSE CONCLUSION

The current evidence appears to support a broad sequence of events but does not conclusively establish a single precise minute-by-minute timeline.

Significant uncertainty remains regarding:

* Exact time of death
* Reliability of witness timing estimates
* Interpretation of limousine observations
* Meaning of reported sounds
* Chronological significance of biological evidence
* Chronological significance of glove evidence

Accordingly, the defense position is that the prosecution timeline contains numerous inferential steps that should be carefully examined and tested before being accepted as an established factual chronology.
