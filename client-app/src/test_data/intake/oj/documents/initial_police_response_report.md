# INCIDENT SUMMARY

On June 13, 1994, at approximately 12:18 a.m., officers responded to reports regarding two apparently deceased individuals located in the exterior walkway area of a residential property located at 875 South Bundy Drive, Los Angeles, California.

Upon arrival, responding officers observed two adult victims lying in close proximity to one another near the front entrance walkway of the residence. Both individuals appeared to have sustained extensive traumatic injuries. Immediate assessment indicated no signs of life.

The scene was secured pending arrival of homicide investigators, forensic personnel, and medical examiner representatives.

The investigation was subsequently classified as a homicide investigation.

---

# INITIAL DISPATCH INFORMATION

### Dispatch Time

* Call Received: Approximately 12:12 a.m.
* Officers Dispatched: Approximately 12:14 a.m.
* First Unit Arrival: Approximately 12:18 a.m.

### Dispatch Narrative

Communications personnel advised responding officers that a concerned individual had reported what appeared to be an unconscious person near the entrance of the residence.

Dispatch logs indicate uncertainty regarding the nature of the incident during the initial call. Early communications referenced a possible medical emergency before responding officers identified evidence of violent trauma.

# SCENE CONDITIONS

### Weather

* Clear skies
* Mild temperature
* No active precipitation
* Dry pavement surfaces

### Lighting Conditions

* Exterior residential lighting present
* Additional illumination provided by responding officers
* Certain areas of the walkway and landscaping were partially shadowed

### General Scene Appearance

Responding officers observed:

* Significant amounts of apparent blood on the walkway
* Blood staining extending across portions of the entry path
* Blood transfer patterns on nearby surfaces
* Apparent signs of a violent altercation

No obvious signs of forced entry into the residence were observed during the initial walkthrough of exterior areas.

Officers did not immediately determine whether the incident occurred entirely at the observed location or whether any victim may have been moved.

# SCENE SECURITY ACTIONS

Upon confirmation of apparent homicide conditions, officers implemented scene preservation procedures.

Actions included:

* Establishing outer perimeter
* Establishing inner crime scene perimeter
* Restricting entry access
* Maintaining scene log
* Separating potential witnesses
* Preventing contamination by unauthorized personnel

The following categories of individuals were permitted access:

* Patrol supervisors
* Detectives
* Crime scene investigators
* Coroner personnel
* Authorized forensic personnel

A preliminary access log was initiated.

# PHYSICAL OBSERVATIONS

Responding officers documented the following observations prior to forensic processing.

### Blood Evidence

Officers observed:

* Large concentrations of blood near both victims
* Blood staining on walkway surfaces
* Apparent blood droplets extending beyond primary body locations
* Possible transfer patterns near gate and pathway areas

At the time of initial response, officers could not determine:

* Source attribution of observed blood
* Sequence of deposition
* Whether all blood originated from the victims

---

### Personal Property

Observed items included:

* Eyeglasses near victim area
* Personal effects associated with victims
* Various items requiring forensic documentation

Officers were instructed not to move or inspect items beyond visual identification.

---

### Footwear and Impression Evidence

Responding officers observed what appeared to be:

* Multiple impressions in the vicinity of the scene
* Potential footwear impressions in blood-containing areas

Officers noted that impression evidence appeared vulnerable to contamination due to necessary emergency access routes.

Photographic documentation was requested immediately.

# PRELIMINARY CANVASS OF AREA

Responding officers conducted a limited neighborhood canvass while maintaining scene security.

Several nearby residents reported:

* Hearing a dog barking repeatedly
* Observing unusual activity involving a dog moving through the neighborhood
* Noticing activity later than normal for the area

Witness recollections varied regarding timing.

Reported estimates ranged from approximately 10:45 p.m. through shortly after midnight.

Investigators noted that witness timing estimates would require verification.

# EMERGING INVESTIGATIVE INFORMATION

During the early stages of the investigation, responding officers became aware of prior domestic disturbance history involving Nicole Brown Simpson and former spouse Orenthal James Simpson.

This information was communicated to detectives for follow-up investigation.

At the time of this report:

* No arrest had been made
* No suspect had been formally charged
* Investigative leads remained active

Officers were instructed not to draw conclusions regarding suspect identity prior to completion of forensic processing and witness interviews.

---

# EVIDENCE PRESERVATION NOTES

Responding officers documented several concerns relevant to future forensic review:

### Potential Contamination Risks

* Multiple responding personnel required scene access
* Medical personnel entered portions of the scene
* Lighting limitations existed during initial response
* Certain areas were traversed before full perimeter establishment

### Documentation Requirements

Investigators recommended:

* Comprehensive chain-of-custody tracking
* Detailed photographic logging
* Independent verification of collection locations
* Reconstruction of scene access chronology

These recommendations were documented due to the anticipated evidentiary significance of biological evidence present at the scene.

---

# OPEN QUESTIONS IDENTIFIED DURING INITIAL RESPONSE

The following issues remained unresolved at the conclusion of patrol response operations:

1. Exact time of death for each victim.
2. Whether one or multiple assailants were involved.
3. Whether the attack occurred entirely at the observed location.
4. Source attribution of all blood evidence present.
5. Meaning and significance of observed footwear impressions.
6. Whether any witness directly observed the attack.
7. Precise timeline of events between approximately 9:30 p.m. and midnight.
8. Whether any suspect sustained injuries during the incident.
9. Whether additional crime scenes or evidence locations existed.
10. Whether neighborhood witness accounts could be reconciled into a consistent timeline.

---

# TRANSFER OF INVESTIGATION

At approximately 2:03 a.m., responsibility for the investigation was formally transferred to homicide detectives and crime scene personnel.

The scene remained secured pending:

* Full forensic processing
* Coroner examination
* Evidence collection
* Expanded witness interviews
* Timeline reconstruction

Patrol officers remained available for supplemental reports and follow-up witness identification.
