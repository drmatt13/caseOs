# PRELIMINARY CASE ASSESSMENT

The available records suggest a relationship marked by both conflict and continued interaction over an extended period.

While certain incidents may be viewed as favorable to the prosecution's motive theory, many records contain unresolved factual disputes, incomplete documentation, differing witness accounts, and events that occurred years before the charged offenses.

Accordingly, each incident should be evaluated individually rather than treated as proof of a single continuous pattern.
