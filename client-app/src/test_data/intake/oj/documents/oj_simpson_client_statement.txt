================================================================================
IMPORTANT NOTE
==============

This document reflects statements provided by the client during meetings with
defense counsel and investigators.

The contents represent the client's recollection of events and should not be
treated as independently verified facts.

The statement is intended to assist counsel in understanding the client's
position regarding:

* Relationship history
* Activities on June 12, 1994
* Travel plans
* Timeline issues
* Response to allegations

This document is attorney work product and subject to revision.

================================================================================
BACKGROUND
==========

My name is Orenthal James Simpson.

I was married to Nicole Brown Simpson and we had two children together.

Although our marriage ended in divorce, Nicole remained an important part of my
life because of our children and our shared history.

Like many divorced couples, we had disagreements over the years.

Sometimes those disagreements became emotional.

At other times we got along well.

Our relationship was complicated and changed over time.

I do not agree with efforts to describe the relationship using only the worst
moments while ignoring years of family interaction, shared parenting, holidays,
birthdays, and continued contact.

================================================================================
RELATIONSHIP WITH NICOLE
========================

After our divorce, we remained involved in one another's lives because of our
children.

We attended family events.

We communicated regularly.

Sometimes we argued.

Sometimes we got along.

There were periods where we were closer and periods where we were more distant.

I understand that prosecutors may attempt to portray our relationship as one of
constant hostility.

That is not how I experienced it.

There were disagreements, but there were also many ordinary interactions that
never appear in police reports.

================================================================================
PRIOR INCIDENTS
===============

I understand that prior police contacts may be introduced during this case.

I am not claiming that every interaction between Nicole and me was perfect.

There were arguments.

There were emotional situations.

There were times when police became involved.

However, I do not believe those incidents accurately define our entire
relationship.

I expect the prosecution will attempt to use prior incidents to argue motive.

I disagree with the suggestion that prior disagreements prove involvement in
what happened on June 12, 1994.

================================================================================
JUNE 12, 1994
EARLIER IN THE DAY
==================

My recollection is that June 12 began as a relatively ordinary day.

I spent time engaged in normal personal activities.

I was preparing for a scheduled trip to Chicago.

The trip had been planned in advance.

Travel arrangements had already been made.

I expected to leave later that evening.

I had no reason to believe anything unusual would occur.

================================================================================
FAMILY ACTIVITIES
=================

I was aware of family activities involving Nicole and members of her family.

Nothing about those activities caused concern.

There was no confrontation.

There was no event that would have led me to believe violence was about to
occur.

================================================================================
TRAVEL PREPARATION
==================

A significant portion of my evening involved preparing for travel.

I knew I had a flight to catch.

I knew transportation had been arranged.

I packed luggage and prepared for departure.

My focus was on making my scheduled flight.

People familiar with air travel understand that preparing for a late-night
flight involves numerous routine activities.

================================================================================
ROCKINGHAM RESIDENCE
====================

During the evening I remained primarily at my residence.

I was preparing for the upcoming trip.

I interacted with individuals at the property.

Nothing about the evening seemed extraordinary at the time.

I did not travel to Nicole's residence.

I was not present at Bundy Drive during the time prosecutors allege the crimes
occurred.

================================================================================
KATO KAELIN
===========

I recall interacting with Kato Kaelin during portions of the evening.

The interactions were routine.

Nothing occurred that would have suggested a violent event had taken place
elsewhere.

To my knowledge, Kato's observations relate primarily to hearing a sound near
the guest house area.

I cannot explain what produced that sound.

================================================================================
ALLAN PARK ARRIVAL
==================

I knew a limousine driver was scheduled to transport me to Los Angeles
International Airport.

Eventually the driver arrived.

Travel preparations continued.

I gathered luggage and prepared to leave.

Nothing about the airport trip was unusual from my perspective.

================================================================================
AIRPORT DEPARTURE
=================

I departed for Los Angeles International Airport according to previously
scheduled travel arrangements.

The trip itself was routine.

My intention was to board my scheduled flight to Chicago.

At that point I had no knowledge of the events that had occurred at Nicole's
residence.

================================================================================
KNOWLEDGE OF THE HOMICIDES
==========================

I did not learn of Nicole's death until later.

The news was shocking.

Regardless of our history, Nicole was the mother of my children.

My reaction was one of disbelief.

I also learned that Ronald Goldman had been killed.

I did not know why such an event would occur.

I had no involvement in either death.

================================================================================
ALLEGATIONS REGARDING BLOOD EVIDENCE
====================================

I understand that biological evidence has become a major focus of the
investigation.

I am not a scientist.

I cannot explain every laboratory conclusion.

I can only state that I did not commit these murders.

Because I deny involvement, I believe the collection, handling, and
interpretation of evidence should be examined carefully.

================================================================================
ALLEGATIONS REGARDING GLOVES
============================

I understand investigators believe glove evidence is important.

I understand there are allegations connecting gloves recovered at different
locations.

I dispute any suggestion that glove evidence alone proves I committed a crime.

I expect experts will evaluate the reliability of the evidence and the methods
used to collect and analyze it.

================================================================================
ALLEGATIONS REGARDING TIMELINE
==============================

I understand prosecutors intend to argue that I had sufficient time to travel,
commit the murders, return home, prepare for travel, and leave for the airport.

I disagree with that conclusion.

I believe the timeline contains assumptions and estimates that are being treated
as established facts.

I expect the timing evidence to be examined closely.

================================================================================
RELATIONSHIP TO RONALD GOLDMAN
==============================

I did not have a significant relationship with Ronald Goldman.

I was aware of him but did not view him as an enemy.

I had no reason to harm him.

I had no involvement in his death.

================================================================================
PUBLIC ATTENTION
================

The level of media attention surrounding this case has been overwhelming.

Stories have circulated before all facts have been determined.

Many people appear to have reached conclusions before hearing all of the
evidence.

I hope the case will ultimately be decided based upon facts rather than public
pressure.

================================================================================
DENIAL OF INVOLVEMENT
=====================

I categorically deny murdering Nicole Brown Simpson.

I categorically deny murdering Ronald Goldman.

I categorically deny participating in any attack upon either individual.

I did not travel to Bundy Drive for the purpose alleged by the prosecution.

I did not commit the crimes charged.

================================================================================
ISSUES I BELIEVE REQUIRE INVESTIGATION
======================================

1. Accuracy of the timeline.
2. Reliability of witness timing estimates.
3. Collection and handling of biological evidence.
4. Chain-of-custody procedures.
5. Interpretation of mixed DNA samples.
6. Circumstances surrounding glove evidence.
7. Credibility of investigative personnel.
8. Alternative explanations for physical evidence.
9. Completeness of police documentation.
10. Media influence on witness recollections.

================================================================================
FINAL STATEMENT
===============

I understand the seriousness of the charges against me.

I understand that Nicole Brown Simpson and Ronald Goldman lost their lives and
that their families deserve answers.

However, I maintain that I am not responsible for these deaths.

I ask that the evidence be examined carefully, that assumptions be challenged,
and that conclusions be based upon facts rather than speculation.

I deny any involvement in the murders and intend to defend myself against the
charges brought by the State.

================================================================================
