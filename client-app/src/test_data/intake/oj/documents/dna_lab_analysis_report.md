# PURPOSE OF EXAMINATION

The Scientific Investigation Division received multiple biological evidence items for DNA characterization and comparison.

The purpose of the examination was to:

* Determine whether biological material recovered from submitted evidence contained human blood.
* Develop DNA profiles from suitable samples.
* Compare evidentiary samples to known reference samples.
* Assess whether biological material recovered from evidence items could be associated with identified individuals.
* Document findings and provide statistical estimates regarding observed DNA profile matches.

# MIXTURE ANALYSIS SUMMARY

Several evidence items contained biological material from multiple contributors.

Mixture interpretation was performed according to laboratory procedures current at the time of analysis.

Analysts considered:

* Relative band intensity
* Signal strength
* Contributor probability
* Population frequency estimates

Mixture interpretation necessarily involves analyst judgment regarding contributor assignment.

Independent review was conducted.

Reviewing analyst agreed with primary interpretations.

---

# STATISTICAL ANALYSIS

Population frequency calculations were performed using laboratory reference databases.

Estimated frequencies varied by evidence item.

Observed profiles were generally considered highly uncommon.

Laboratory calculations produced statistical estimates ranging from:

* One in several thousand
* One in several million
* One in hundreds of millions

depending upon profile completeness and population group assumptions.

Complete calculations appear in attached worksheets.

# PROSECUTION-ORIENTED FORENSIC CONCLUSIONS

Based upon the submitted evidence and testing performed, the laboratory reached the following conclusions.

### Conclusion 1

Biological material consistent with Orenthal James Simpson was identified at multiple evidentiary locations associated with the investigation.

---

### Conclusion 2

Biological material consistent with Nicole Brown Simpson and Ronald Goldman was identified on evidence items associated with both the Bundy crime scene and evidence recovered from the Rockingham property.

---

### Conclusion 3

Mixed biological evidence recovered from the glove evidence contained profile characteristics consistent with all three referenced individuals.

---

### Conclusion 4

The observed DNA findings are generally consistent with investigative information suggesting physical association between the victims, crime scene evidence, and items recovered during the investigation.
