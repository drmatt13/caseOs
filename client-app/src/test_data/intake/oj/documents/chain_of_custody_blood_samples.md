# DOCUMENT PURPOSE

This document summarizes the collection, transfer, storage, laboratory submission, testing requests, and chain-of-custody history for biological evidence believed to contain blood recovered during the investigation.

The document consolidates information from:

* Crime scene collection logs
* Evidence property reports
* Detective supplemental reports
* Laboratory submission forms
* Coroner evidence transfer receipts
* Reference sample collection records

This report is intended as an administrative tracking document and is not a laboratory interpretation report.

# CHAIN OF CUSTODY AUDIT OBSERVATIONS

Evidence auditors identified several issues requiring clarification.

---

## Observation 1

Certain photographic references associated with biological samples contain numbering inconsistencies.

Issue appears administrative.

Impact undetermined.

---

## Observation 2

Several evidence transfer sheets contain handwritten modifications.

Most corrections were initialed.

At least one correction lacked accompanying initials.

Further explanation not located.

---

## Observation 3

Reference blood sample documentation for Orenthal James Simpson reflects differing recorded volumes between collection and laboratory intake records.

Investigators subsequently requested clarification.

No consolidated explanation appears in reviewed administrative materials.

---

## Observation 4

Multiple evidence items were transported together during certain transfers.

Evidence seals appear documented as intact.

However, transportation logs do not always specify exact container placement during transit.

---

## Observation 5

Time gaps ranging from approximately 30 minutes to several hours exist between collection and formal evidence booking for various items.

Reviewers noted these intervals are not necessarily unusual but should be considered when reconstructing evidence handling chronology.
