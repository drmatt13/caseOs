# INVESTIGATIVE NOTES

Vehicle examiners documented several matters requiring follow-up:

### Issue A

Whether biological material observed inside the vehicle originated from a single event or multiple unrelated events.

### Issue B

Whether trace evidence recovered from seating surfaces can be linked to any specific individual.

### Issue C

Whether receipt timestamps and document records assist in reconstructing movements during the relevant time period.

### Issue D

Whether any biological material was transferred indirectly through secondary contact rather than direct deposition.

# OPEN QUESTIONS

1. When were the observed biological stains deposited?
2. Did all biological evidence originate from the same incident?
3. What significance, if any, should be attributed to trace evidence recovered from vehicle seating areas?
4. Can latent prints be conclusively attributed to specific individuals?
5. Can recovered receipts assist in establishing a reliable timeline?
6. Are all vehicle evidence locations represented in both photographs and diagrams?
7. Were any stains present before the events under investigation?
