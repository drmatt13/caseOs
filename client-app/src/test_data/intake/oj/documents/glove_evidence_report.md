# PURPOSE OF REPORT

This report documents the discovery, collection, handling, examination, and evidentiary significance of glove-related evidence recovered during the investigation.

The report summarizes observations made by responding investigators, crime scene personnel, forensic analysts, and evidence custodians concerning two gloves believed by investigators to be associated with the same pair.

This report does not contain ultimate conclusions regarding criminal responsibility and should be read in conjunction with:

* DNA Laboratory Analysis Report
* Biological Evidence Chain-of-Custody Report
* Crime Scene Photography Log
* Rockingham Search Report
* Trace Fiber Analysis Report
* Timeline Reconstruction Memorandum

# DOCUMENTATION ISSUES IDENTIFIED

## Issue 1

Several early reports describe the Bundy glove as being partially concealed.

Later summaries characterize the glove as plainly visible.

Investigators did not reconcile the differing descriptions.

---

## Issue 2

Initial scene sketches and later evidence diagrams depict slightly different glove orientations.

Difference may be attributable to drawing approximation rather than actual movement.

Issue noted during review.

---

## Issue 3

Photographic numbering references differ between:

* Crime Scene Log
* Evidence Collection Sheet
* Laboratory Intake Form

No indication that the glove itself was misidentified.

Administrative clarification requested.

---

## Issue 4

Certain notes describing glove condition were prepared several hours after discovery rather than contemporaneously at collection.

This timing discrepancy was noted during evidence review.

---

# GLOVE FIT DEMONSTRATION HISTORY

## Investigative Note

Investigators anticipated that glove ownership could become a disputed issue.

Several discussions occurred regarding whether demonstrative fitting procedures should be performed.

At the time of this report, no formal evidentiary conclusions regarding fit had been reached.

---

## Considerations Identified

Potential variables affecting fit include:

* Leather shrinkage
* Moisture exposure
* Drying process
* Presence of liners
* Use of protective gloves beneath evidence gloves
* Age and condition of materials

No final conclusions documented in this report.

# POTENTIAL DEFENSE ISSUES IDENTIFIED DURING REVIEW

Evidence review personnel noted several areas likely to receive scrutiny.

### Issue A

Whether both gloves can be conclusively established as originating from the same pair.

### Issue B

Whether biological material could have been transferred through indirect mechanisms.

### Issue C

Whether collection and storage procedures eliminated contamination concerns.

### Issue D

Whether documentation discrepancies affect reliability of evidentiary interpretation.

### Issue E

Whether glove condition changed between recovery and later examination.

### Issue F

Whether environmental exposure affected evidence quality.

---

# OPEN QUESTIONS

1. When and how were the gloves separated?
2. Were both gloves deposited during the same event?
3. Can ownership be established independently of biological evidence?
4. Did environmental exposure alter the condition of either glove?
5. Were all collection photographs accurately cross-referenced?
6. Can the complete chain of custody be reconstructed without ambiguity?
7. What role, if any, did indirect transfer play in the observed biological findings?
8. Do manufacturing records exist identifying purchasers of similar gloves?

---

# INVESTIGATOR SUMMARY

Investigators consider the glove evidence to be a potentially significant component of the overall case because it may provide a physical connection between locations, individuals, and biological evidence identified during the investigation.

However, questions remain regarding collection chronology, documentation consistency, interpretation of biological findings, and the significance of the gloves independent of other evidence.

These issues remain subject to further forensic analysis, expert review, and litigation.
