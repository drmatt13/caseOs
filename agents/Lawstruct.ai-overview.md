# Lawstruct.ai

## Overview

Lawstruct.ai is an AI-powered legal case intelligence platform that transforms unstructured case materials into a structured, interconnected representation of a legal matter.

Rather than treating a case as a collection of documents, Lawstruct models a case as a network of facts, claims, issues, arguments, evidence, events, people, and supporting materials. The platform helps attorneys organize information, understand relationships, identify supporting evidence, and reason about a case at a higher level than traditional document management or legal search tools.

The goal is to create a living, evolving digital representation of a case that can be explored by both attorneys and AI agents.

---

# The Problem

Legal information is fragmented across:

- Pleadings
- Discovery
- Depositions
- Contracts
- Emails
- Exhibits
- Photos
- Expert reports
- Attorney notes
- Research materials

Attorneys spend significant time manually:

- Reviewing documents
- Building timelines
- Connecting evidence to claims
- Tracking witnesses and testimony
- Identifying supporting and conflicting facts
- Maintaining case strategy

Most legal AI systems rely on document retrieval and chat interfaces, forcing users to repeatedly search through the same information.

Lawstruct aims to transform case information into structured knowledge that persists and improves over time.

---

# Core Concept

Every case is represented as a collection of interconnected records.

Examples include:

- Case Summary
- Objectives
- Claims
- Issues
- Theories
- Arguments
- Facts
- Timeline Events
- Testimony
- Legal Precedents
- Notes
- Documents
- People

Each record can reference other records through explicit relationships.

Examples:

- A fact supports a claim
- Testimony corroborates a fact
- A document contains testimony
- A timeline event involves a person
- An issue impacts an objective
- An argument relies on legal precedent

Together these records form a case knowledge graph.

---

# AI-Assisted Case Construction

When documents are uploaded:

1. Documents are analyzed.
2. Relevant information is extracted.
3. Existing case context is reviewed.
4. New records are proposed.
5. Relationships between records are suggested.
6. Users review and approve changes.

The AI functions as a case analyst that continuously helps organize and expand the case model.

---

# Human-In-The-Loop Design

Lawstruct is designed around attorney oversight.

AI does not automatically become the source of truth.

Instead:

- AI proposes records.
- AI proposes relationships.
- AI proposes updates.
- Attorneys review and approve changes.

This creates an auditable and trustworthy workflow while reducing manual effort.

---

# Case Intelligence Layer

The platform is designed to provide a higher level of reasoning than traditional document search.

Users should be able to ask questions such as:

- What facts support this claim?
- Which witnesses support this argument?
- What evidence contradicts this theory?
- What events occurred before this incident?
- Which documents support this issue?
- What legal precedents are associated with this argument?

Answers are generated from structured case knowledge rather than document retrieval alone.

---

# Timeline Intelligence

Lawstruct automatically builds and maintains case timelines.

Timeline events can be linked to:

- Documents
- People
- Facts
- Testimony
- Claims
- Evidence

This allows attorneys to understand sequences of events, identify gaps, and analyze causation.

---

# Knowledge Graph Approach

The platform is built around the belief that legal matters are networks of relationships rather than collections of files.

Documents are considered source material.

The primary asset is the structured case knowledge extracted from those documents.

This enables:

- Better case understanding
- Faster information retrieval
- More accurate AI reasoning
- Reduced duplication of work
- Persistent case memory

---

# Long-Term Vision

Lawstruct aims to become a legal operating system for case analysis and management.

The platform's long-term goal is to create a comprehensive digital representation of a case where:

- Documents become structured records
- Records become interconnected knowledge
- Knowledge becomes case intelligence
- AI agents assist attorneys with analysis, organization, and reasoning

The result is a system that helps legal professionals understand complex matters faster, maintain strategic awareness, and make better-informed decisions throughout the lifecycle of a case.
